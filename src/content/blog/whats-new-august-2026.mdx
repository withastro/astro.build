### For you, from the team

There's a new place for asking, searching (and answering!) Astro support questions. In addition to our Discord support forums, you can now visit our [Community Support repo on GitHub](https://github.com/withastro/community-support/discussions) and participate in the Discussion! Thanks <Mention name="florian" / > for kicking off this community project!

Have you ever wanted to write an Astro component without making a whole new project? Try the new [Astro Playground](https://play.astro.build/) from <Mention name="ema" />. Under the hood, every playground session spins up its own real Astro Compiler instance in a Dynamic Worker, so what you see is exactly what you'd get locally. Built with Svelte for the UI.

## Tips & Tools

- [starlight to skills](https://github.com/HiDeoo/starlight-to-skills/): Turn Starlight documentation pages into reviewed, discoverable, and up-to-date agent skills.
